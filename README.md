# Mixer.fund

Mixer is a social index platform on Solana that turns investment theses into diversified, on-chain portfolios.

**Everyone has a thesis. Mixer lets you put it to work.**

Crypto has always been driven by narratives.

AI. DePIN. Memecoins. Privacy. Infrastructure. Now, tokenized equities are bringing traditional companies onto Solana.

But when you have a thesis today, you still have to express it the hard way.

- You find the assets.
- You decide the weights.
- You buy them one by one.
- You manage the positions.
- And if someone disagrees with your thesis, they usually just post about it.

Mixer thinks, a thesis should be something you can build, invest in, and compete with.

## What Mixer does?

Mixer lets anyone turn an investment thesis into a diversified index basket of tokenized equities and Solana assets.

A creator might believe that AI infrastructure is about to explode.

Instead of posting a watchlist, they create:

**AI Infrastructure**
- NVIDIA — 30%; AMD — 20%; Palantir — 50%

They commit their own capital, publish the Mixer, and let the market decide.

A user who believes in the thesis doesn't need to buy five different assets and manage five different positions. They just buy the Mixer. **One position. One chart. One thesis.**

But here's where it gets interesting. Mixer isn't just a portfolio builder. It's a competitive social layer for investment ideas.
- See a Mixer you disagree with? **Remix it.**
- Think the creator got the allocation wrong? **Build your own version.**
- Think your thesis is better? **Challenge it.**

Two creators can start with the same narrative and construct completely different portfolios. Their performance becomes the scoreboard.

Instead of:
> "I think AI is going to outperform."

You get:
> "Here's my thesis. Here's my allocation. I put my own capital behind it. Now let's see."

Creators earn fees from their indexes and build a public performance history around their ideas.
Users can discover Mixers, follow creators, compare portfolios, and decide which theses they want to back.

The result is a new loop:
**Create → Invest → Compete → Perform → Remix**

## Fast-Paced Markets, Made Fun

Mixer isn't limited to long-term investment theses.

The same idea can make fast-moving memecoin markets more social and competitive.

Creators can build time-bound Meme Mixers around a moment, narrative, or category — for example, a basket of the hottest AI memecoins for the next 24 hours, or a collection of tokens competing around a trending narrative.

Instead of chasing and trading each token individually, users can enter the entire bucket as one position.

The clock is part of the game.

A Mixer can launch, attract capital, compete against other Mixers, and expire after a defined period. Creators can experiment with different allocations, users can back the ones they believe in, and performance becomes the scoreboard.

Same narrative. Different thesis. One winner.

This gives Mixer a more playful side: a place where creators can turn the chaos and speed of memecoin markets into structured, social, competitive investment products.

## Built for Solana

Underneath the social experience is an on-chain portfolio primitive.
Mixers use program-controlled vaults through PDAs, allowing the protocol to enforce portfolio rules without requiring creators to custody user funds.

Asset composition and allocation weights can be transparently represented on-chain, giving every Mixer a verifiable structure and performance history.

Mixer has potential to expand into RWAs.

## Where We Are

We already have a working early MVP running on Solana devnet.

Today, you can:

- Create a Mixer
- Select assets
- Define allocations
- Visualize unified performance
- Explore the Mixer experience

We're now building the on-chain execution layer that turns the experience into a real investment primitive: program-controlled vaults, deposits and withdrawals, asset execution, creator fees, and the full Mixer lifecycle.

## The Bigger Idea

Tokenized equities put individual stocks on-chain.

Mixer puts the portfolio on-chain.

But we're taking it one step further.

We want portfolios to become social objects — things people create, discover, invest in, compete with, and remix.

Because the most interesting part of investing isn't just what you buy.

**It's why you believe in it.**

Build your thesis. Put capital behind it. Let the market decide.
